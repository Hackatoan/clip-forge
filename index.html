Clip Forge — Free Browser Video Editor

Clip Forge is a free, full-featured video editor that runs entirely in your web browser. Trim, cut, and arrange clips on a multi-track timeline, add keyframe animation, GPU green-screen, filters, transitions and audio — then export up to 4K or 8K. Nothing is uploaded: your footage never leaves your machine, and there is no account, no watermark, and no install.

How to edit video in your browser

  1. Open the editor — it loads instantly in any modern desktop browser, no sign-up.
  2. Drop in your clips — drag videos, audio, images, or whole folders onto the timeline.
  3. Edit — trim, split, snap and layer clips; add keyframes, chroma key, filters, transitions and audio fades.
  4. Export — render to WebM or H.264 MP4 from 480p up to 4K and 8K.

Features

  • Multi-track timeline — video, audio, images, text & shapes on unlimited layers.
  • Keyframe animation — opacity, scale, position, rotation and volume with eased interpolation.
  • GPU chroma key — green/blue-screen removal in a WebGL shader.
  • Filters & blend modes — brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, plus multiply/screen/overlay.
  • Transitions & crossfades — fades, zooms, slides and true cross-clip dissolves.
  • Real audio tools — fades, per-clip volume, auto-ducking, reverse and a live master meter.
  • Export up to 8K — WebM or H.264 MP4, bitrate scales with resolution.
  • No upload, no account — everything runs client-side; footage never leaves your machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is Clip Forge free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no account, no watermark, no install, and no upload required.

Do my videos get uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser; your footage never leaves your machine.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Clip Forge runs entirely in your web browser, so there is nothing to download or install.

What can Clip Forge export?

WebM or H.264 MP4 from 480p up to 4K and 8K.
